Nonprofit organizations thrive when they build strong, engaged communities of supporters. Since these communities are rooted in trust, meaningful relationships, and a shared sense of purpose, one of the most effective ways to strengthen those connections is through a thoughtful donor retention strategy. Nonprofit donor retention focuses on keeping contributors engaged year after year.
