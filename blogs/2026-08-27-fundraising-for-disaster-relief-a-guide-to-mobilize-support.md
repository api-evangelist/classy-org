When a disaster happens, nonprofits are often the first to mobilize and the last to leave. They step in to provide urgent relief, such as food, clean water, shelter, and medical care. Equally critical, they remain on the ground long after the headlines fade, helping communities rebuild, restoring infrastructure, and supporting long-term recovery.
