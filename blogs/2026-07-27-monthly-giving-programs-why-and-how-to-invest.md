Building a strong recurring giving program is no longer a nice-to-have but critical to nonprofits’ sustainability and scalability. Charitable dollars increased 5% in 2025, the strongest growth rate in five years, according to the Fundraising Effectiveness Project. Yet, donor count fell by 3.6%, meaning fewer donors gave.
