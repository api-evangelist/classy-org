Acquiring new donors is an investment. It can cost nonprofits $1.50 to raise $1.00 from a new supporter. Additionally, economic uncertainty, changes in donor behaviors, and a shift in trust from institutions to individuals emphasize how critical it is for nonprofits to adapt their strategies to align with today’s best practices.
