Successful fundraising is about more than asking for donations. In fact, the strongest nonprofits draw from multiple revenue sources, including individual giving, grants, corporate partnerships, events, and more. A diversified funding model helps organizations stay resilient when one revenue stream shifts or declines, making it easier to sustain programs and plan for long-term growth.
