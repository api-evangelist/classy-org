Donation tax receipts do two things: help supporters keep clear records of their gifts and help nonprofits maintain accurate financial documentation. Clear, consistent receipts confirm correctly processed donations and give supporters the information they may need for year-end tax records. They also support organized bookkeeping, greater financial transparency, and tax-exempt compliance.
